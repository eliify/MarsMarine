MarsMarine
UE5 Game with Blueprints.

  This is a game I made following the Udemy course [Unreal Engine Blueprint Game Developer.](https://www.udemy.com/course/unrealblueprint/)

Requirements
Unreal Engine 5.5.4

Survive relentless waves of alien invaders!
Enemies will swarm the map in increasingly dangerous waves—your only goal is to stay alive. Aim with your mouse and fire with the left mouse button. Move using the WASD keys for precision control.
Prefer a gamepad? Use the right thumbstick to aim and the right trigger to shoot. The left thumbstick lets you maneuver around the battlefield with ease.

After each wave, prepare for greater challenges as the next one brings tougher enemies and faster action. Health pickups will occasionally appear—grab them to restore your health. But be quick: as the game gets harder, these lifesavers take longer to return.

Stay within the mission zone and keep your health up—step out of bounds or run out of health, and it’s game over.
